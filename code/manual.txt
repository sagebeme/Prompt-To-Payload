Mawingu Tech Staff Handbook (fictional company, for course exercises)

Working hours
Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your own schedule, as long as you work 40 hours a week. Team leads must approve any regular schedule that starts after 10:00.

Remote work
Staff may work remotely up to 3 days a week. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from your manager and HR. When working remotely you must be reachable on Slack during core hours.

Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 21 working days of paid annual leave per year. Leave requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; the rest expire on 31 January.

Sick leave
You get 14 days of fully paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, a doctor's note is required. Tell your manager before 10:00 on any day you are off sick.

Parental leave
Mothers receive 3 months of fully paid maternity leave. Fathers receive 2 weeks of fully paid paternity leave. Either parent can request 4 additional weeks of unpaid leave.

Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop. You are responsible for keeping it secure. Report lost or stolen equipment to IT within 24 hours. Personal software must not be installed without IT approval.

Expenses
Work expenses up to KES 5,000 can be claimed with a receipt through the finance portal. Expenses above KES 5,000 need your manager's approval before you spend the money. Claims must be submitted within 30 days.

Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of KES 60,000 per year for courses, books and conferences related to their role. Unused budget does not carry over.

Security
Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff. IT will never ask for it. Use the company password manager. Report suspicious emails to security@mawingu.example by forwarding them, then delete them.
